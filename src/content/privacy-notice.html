TARS AI Privacy Notice

URL: https://tarsai.co/privacy Version: 1.0 Effective Date: June 12, 2026 Last Updated: June 12, 2026

TARS AI Inc. ("TARS," "we," "us") provides an operations platform for autonomous-vehicle depot and field operations to business customers. This notice explains what personal information we process, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights available to you.

The short version: we process a minimized set of workplace information about the personnel of our business customers, solely to provide the platform to those customers. We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

1. Who this notice covers

  • Platform users: employees and contractors of our business customers who use the TARS platform as part of their work. For this data, TARS generally acts as a service provider / processor on behalf of the customer (your employer or its client), which is the controller.
  • Website visitors: people who visit tarsai.co.
  • Business contacts: people who correspond with us in a business context.

2. Personal information we collect

For platform users, we collect only the workplace information the service needs. Described by the categories used in California privacy law:

Category What it includes
Identifiers Name, work email address, employee ID, and IP address
Professional or employment-related information General workforce information, such as job role and work assignments, and work-related performance information
Authentication credentials Account password, stored only as a salted bcrypt hash (never in plain text)
Geolocation data Device location of field personnel, collected only during active work tasks
Internet or other electronic network activity Browser type, timestamps, and application and access logs
Visual information Photographs submitted as part of incident reports

What we do not do, and do not collect:

  • We do not record or store audio. Where the platform supports dictation, speech is converted to text on your device and only the resulting text is retained.
  • We do not collect government identifiers (Social Security, driver's license, or passport numbers), financial or payment information, biometric data, health information, data about members of the public, or passenger data belonging to our customers' clients.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies, pixels, or beacons. The application uses browser storage to keep you signed in and a single functional cookie for interface preferences.

3. Where this information comes from

  • Directly from you, when you or your colleagues use the platform.
  • From your employer (our customer), when your account is provisioned.
  • Generated by the platform itself (timestamps, logs, and work-related metrics).

We do not buy personal information, scrape external sources, or obtain data about you from data brokers.

4. Why we process it (purposes and lawful bases)

Purpose Lawful basis (GDPR/UK GDPR)
Providing and operating the platform for our business customers Performance of our contract with your employer; contractual necessity in the employment context
Security, fraud prevention, audit logging, and incident response Legitimate interests in securing the service; legal obligation where applicable
Support and troubleshooting Performance of contract; legitimate interests
Compliance with law, regulation, or legal process Legal obligation

We do not use personal information for marketing, profiling for advertising, or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. The platform includes AI-assisted features to help our customers work more efficiently; these features are advisory only, and a human remains responsible for any consequential decision.

AI and your data: we use AI service providers to power in-product assistance and search. Where personal information is processed by these features, it is used only to produce the immediate result for that request. Personal information is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model, ours or anyone else's.

5. Who we share it with (sub-processors)

We share personal information only with the categories of service providers needed to run the platform:

Category of provider Purpose Location
Cloud infrastructure provider Hosting, database, storage, and backup United States
AI service providers Power in-product assistance and search features United States

These providers act on our instructions under contract and are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations. AI service providers process personal information only to return the immediate result and do not use it to train or improve any model. A current list of our sub-processors is available to customers on request.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or subpoena; where lawful, we notify the customer (controller) before complying.

We do not sell or share personal information. TARS does not sell personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, Cal. Civ. Code 1798.140(ad)) and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by the CPRA (1798.140(ah)). Because we do not sell or share personal information, a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is not required and is not provided; this statement is the operative disclosure.

6. How long we keep it (retention)

Data category Retention
Active account data Duration of the service relationship
Deactivated accounts 90 days, then permanent deletion
Task and operational records 2 years
Exception and incident records 3 years
Incident media (photos) 1 year after resolution
Sign-in sessions 24 hours (access token); 7 days (refresh token)
Encrypted backups 90-day rolling window
Application audit logs (user actions) 3 years
Security and infrastructure logs (immutable central archive) 7 years

At the end of a customer contract, data is returned in a machine-readable format and/or permanently deleted at the customer's choice, with written confirmation.

When an account is permanently deleted (including deletion requests), any security audit-trail entries that referenced the account are retained for the audit-log retention period with the account identity removed: the record of what action occurred and when is preserved for security and integrity purposes, but it is no longer linked to the deleted person.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights (CCPA 1798.125).

Because TARS processes platform-user data on behalf of your employer (or its client) as the controller, the fastest path is usually to raise a request with your employer, and we will support it: we honor access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection requests routed through the customer within the legally required timelines (for example, 45 days under CCPA and one month under GDPR, subject to permitted extensions).

You may also contact us directly using the methods below, and we will respond or route the request to the responsible controller.

EEA, UK, and Swiss individuals also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

8. International transfers

TARS stores and processes data in the United States. Where personal information of EEA, UK, or Swiss individuals is transferred to the United States, we rely on recognized transfer mechanisms: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where the receiving entity is certified, and Standard Contractual Clauses otherwise, together with the technical and organizational safeguards described in our security documentation.

9. How we protect it

Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 with dedicated managed keys); least-privilege access controls with multi-factor authentication; network segmentation with no public access to application or database servers; continuous security monitoring; and immutable audit logging. Our full security posture is described in our security whitepaper, available to customers on request.

10. Children

The platform is a workplace tool for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this notice

We will post any material change to this page and update the "Last Updated" date. For significant changes affecting platform users, we will also notify our customers so they can inform their personnel.
